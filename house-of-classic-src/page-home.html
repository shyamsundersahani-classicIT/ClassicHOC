Luxury Staying Redefined

House of Classic is a luxury party house, perfect for celebrating all your special occasions - from bachelor’s parties and birthdays to family get-togethers and corporate events. With a set of exemplary amenities, state-of-the-art sound systems, luxurious décor and a welcoming atmosphere, we will make sure that your stay here is beyond perfect.

Bachelor’s Party | Birthday Party | Pool Party | Dance Party | Ladies’ night out | Product Shoot | Kitty Party | Family night out | Get -together | Reunion Party | Corporate Parties & Events | Not for Sagai / Mehandi
